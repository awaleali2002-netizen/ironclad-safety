About Us

Ironclad Safety Consulting Services

Ironclad Safety Consulting Services is committed to creating and maintaining a workplace that’s not only safety conscious, but also health and environmentally responsible. We believe that every incident regardless of the situation requires shared responsibility. Through hazard identification, risk management, consistent training of workers, and continuous improvement, we strive to protect our employees, clients and community while following HSE requirements by Alberta OHS in all situations.

We fully support and promote the Internal Responsibility System (IRS) by encouraging all levels of management and workers to actively participate and do their part in ensuring everyone is working in a safe and healthy environment. Even though employers are held responsible for the safety of their employees, the employees and clients themselves who are engaging in these dangerous conditions also play a role in keeping a safe workplace for each other. By fostering accountability, collaboration, and open communication, we strive to have a company that creates a culture where safety is a core value and a commitment we all strive for.

Supported by our team of 10 professionals who come from different areas of the HSE industry, Ironclad Safety Consulting Services leverages specialized knowledge and experience that plays its own dedicated role in strengthening the IRS system, promoting safe work practices, which helps our clients in various industries achieve the highest standards of workplace safety.

MISSION

To protect people, strengthen businesses, and build safer workplaces through practical, experienced, and uncompromising occupational health and safety solutions.

Iron Clad partners with Alberta employers to identify risk, meet their OHS obligations, develop capable leaders, and turn safety expectations into practical actions that work in the real world.

We believe safety should not be a burden placed on a business—it should be a fundamental part of how a successful business operates.

VISION

To be Alberta’s most trusted occupational health and safety partner, recognized for turning safety knowledge into safer workplaces, stronger businesses, and lasting results.

Iron Clad envisions workplaces where leaders understand that safety is a business responsibility, workers are empowered to recognize hazards and speak up, safety systems are practical and consistently applied, and businesses move beyond compliance toward genuine safety leadership and continuous improvement.
